Privacy approach

Image operations in this release are performed locally in your browser using the browser Canvas API. The image selected for these supported operations is not sent to a Pixora image-processing server.

Some site infrastructure providers may process standard request metadata such as IP address and browser information to deliver and secure the website. If analytics, accounts, cloud storage or third-party AI services are added later, this page must be updated before those features launch.

Do not upload sensitive or confidential images to any online service unless you understand how they are handled.
